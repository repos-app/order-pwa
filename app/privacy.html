  • 勤怠機能で利用者が許可したカメラ画像、位置情報、打刻時刻、距離等

  • 利用規約・プライバシーポリシーへの同意バージョンおよび同意日時

利用目的

サービス提供、本人確認、権限管理、注文・会計等の店舗業務処理、データ保存・バックアップ、問い合わせ対応、不正利用防止・セキュリティ確保、購読権利の確認、法令対応、障害解析、品質改善に利用します。広告目的の追跡や個人情報の販売は行いません。

izakanpai (the “Service”) helps food-service and other businesses manage orders, checkout, inventory, staff, memberships, reservations, and related operations. This policy explains the information handled by the Service and how it is used.

Information we handle

  • Store, owner, and staff names, email addresses, login IDs, roles, authentication/security information, and other account information
  • Orders, payment method (we do not collect payment-card credentials), refunds, inventory, expenses, reservations, memberships, attendance, and audit activity
  • Phone numbers, email addresses, birthdays, allergy information, notes, and other information a store enters about customers, reservation holders, members, or staff
  • Menu, logo, receipt, attendance-evidence, and other images
  • Camera images, location, punch time, distance, and related attendance information when a user permits those functions
  • Access times and technical information needed for device/browser diagnostics, security, and abuse prevention (source information may be salted and hashed for rate limiting)
  • In-app subscription status and a random billing identifier mapped to the store
  • The accepted versions of the Terms and Privacy Policy and the time of acceptance

Purposes

We use information to provide the Service, authenticate users, manage permissions, process store operations, store and back up data, provide support, prevent abuse, protect security, confirm subscription access, meet legal duties, diagnose failures, and improve quality. We do not track users for advertising or sell personal information.

Customer and staff data entered by a store

When a store enters personal data about customers, members, reservation holders, or staff, the store decides the purpose, necessity, retention, and permitted access for that data. Depending on applicable law, the store is responsible for providing required privacy notices and obtaining consent or another lawful basis and for managing access appropriately.

Allergy or health-related information, attendance photos, location data, and similar information require particular care. Stores should enter only what is necessary and must not use such information beyond appropriate purposes. The Service processes this information as needed to provide the functions requested by the store.

Acceptance and consent records

Links to the Terms and this Privacy Policy are presented during store registration, and acceptance is requested. The Service may record the version accepted and the acceptance time. Material changes may be communicated and fresh acceptance may be requested where appropriate.

Service providers

Cloudflare provides hosting, databases, image storage, and system AI processing through Workers AI for features such as inventory AI scanning and automatic translation; RevenueCat manages in-app subscription status; and Resend provides account-verification, support, and configured service email. If a store uses the optional image-search function, image-search terms may be sent to Pixabay. Inventory images or selected text used for AI features are processed through the Service-managed Cloudflare Workers AI system rather than through an AI-provider API key configured by the store.

These providers may process information in countries or regions outside where the user or store is located. We use providers as needed to deliver the Service and subject to applicable legal requirements.
